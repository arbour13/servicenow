Part 2 · Sub-phase card grid — Plan's 6 cards

Today's grid (minmax(190px,1fr)) fits 5 across at desktop width, so Plan's 6th card wraps alone onto its own line. Three ways to fix it:

A · Narrower cards (fixed min-width)

Drop the minimum card width (190px → 150px) so 6 comfortably fit per row at normal desktop widths. Simple CSS-only change; cards are a bit more compact everywhere, and a very large phase would still wrap eventually.

B · Always exactly one row (columns = card count)

The column count is set per-phase to match its own sub-phase count (capped at, say, 7), so a phase's row always fills edge-to-edge with no dangling wrap — card width just varies a little phase to phase (Execute's 2 cards would be wider than Plan's 6). Needs a couple lines of JS, not just CSS.

C · Horizontal scroll strip

Cards stay at their current comfortable width; instead of wrapping, the row scrolls sideways once it overflows. Never a dangling row, but adds a scroll interaction to what's currently a plain grid.
